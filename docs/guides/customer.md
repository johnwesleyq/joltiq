# Customer guide
1. **Get help**: describe the problem. Dictate if your browser supports it. The assistant asks follow-up questions on your ticket.
2. **Permission**: if the assistant needs to work in a web app or on your PC, you'll see *Permission needed*. You choose how long and how far it may go. Nothing happens until you allow it.
3. **Approve steps**: sensitive changes show exactly what will change. Approve or deny each one.
4. **Sign-ins**: if the app asks you to sign in, the assistant pauses. Take control in the live view and use the protected box for passwords and codes. The assistant can't see them.
5. **Stop at any time**: *Emergency stop* on the session page, or STOP on the banner on your PC (or Ctrl+Alt+Shift+F12).
6. A ticket is marked fixed only after an independent check confirms it. Otherwise a technician takes over.
